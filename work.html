05 Controlled labs · independent review

Systems work,
with receipts.

These are small, controlled systems built to answer practical support questions. Each one includes a failure, the evidence used to isolate it, a bounded change, and a separate check that proves what happened next.

All environments are owned labs. Production, enterprise, and Windows claims are stated only where the evidence supports them.
